What is E-Waste?

E-waste means discarded electrical and electronic devices. It includes old computers, mobile phones, televisions, batteries, chargers and other electronic equipment.

📱

Mobile Phones

Old and unused smartphones can become electronic waste.

💻

Computers

Old laptops, desktops and computer parts contribute to e-waste.

🔋

Batteries

Batteries contain materials that can harm the environment.

Effects of E-Waste

🌱

Soil Pollution

Harmful chemicals from electronic waste can contaminate soil.

💧

Water Pollution

Toxic substances can enter rivers and groundwater.

🌫️

Air Pollution

Burning electronic waste can release harmful gases.

👨‍⚕️

Health Problems

Exposure to toxic substances may affect human health.

How Can We Reduce E-Waste?

♻️ Recycle

Send unwanted electronics to authorized recycling centres.

🔧 Repair

Repair electronic devices instead of throwing them away.

🔄 Reuse

Donate or reuse working electronic devices.

🛒 Responsible Buying

Buy durable products and avoid unnecessary upgrades.

Simple E-Waste Tips

✅ Do not throw electronics in regular garbage.

✅ Donate working electronic devices.

✅ Repair devices whenever possible.

✅ Remove personal data before recycling devices.

✅ Use authorized e-waste collection centres.
